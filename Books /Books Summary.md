- [🔵 🔑 Top 5 Key Lessons](#book-1-lessons)

---

<a id="book-1-idea"></a>
### 🟢 💡 One-line Idea

> [!TIP]
> Most forgetting is normal, and you remember more when you pay attention, sleep well, and keep stress low.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-1)

---

<a id="book-1-lessons"></a>
### 🔵 🔑 Top 5 Key Lessons

> [!NOTE]
> 1. **Attention creates memory**
> 2. **Normal forgetting is not a disease**
> 3. **Sleep stores what you learned**
> 4. **Stress blocks recall**
> 5. **Link new facts to what you know**

<details>
<summary>📖 Real-life examples (click to open)</summary>

1. *You forget where you parked because you were on your phone.*
2. *A name on the tip of your tongue usually returns later.*
3. *Sleeping after studying beats an all-nighter.*
4. *You blank in an exam, then remember on the way home.*
5. *Tie a new colleague's name to a vivid picture.*

</details>

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-1)

---

<a id="book-1-plan"></a>
### 🟡 ✅ 7-Day Action Plan

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Five small steps you can do this week.

| Day | Action | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Do one thing at a time when you put something down. | ☐ |
| Day 2 | Pick one fixed spot for keys, wallet and phone. | ☐ |
| Day 3 | Repeat a new person's name aloud when you meet them. | ☐ |
| Day 5 | Sleep 7 to 8 hours after learning something important. | ☐ |
| Day 7 | Walk or exercise 20 to 30 minutes. | ☐ |

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-1)

---

<a id="book-1-habit"></a>
### 🟣 🔁 Daily Habit

> [!TIP]
> When you put down something important, pause 5 seconds and say it aloud: 'Keys are on the shelf.' Tick a calendar.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-1)

---

<a id="book-1-mistake"></a>
### 🔴 ⚠️ Common Mistake

> [!WARNING]
> Panicking at every lapse. Normal forgetting is about details; see a doctor if forgetting regularly disrupts daily life.

```diff
+ Do: Give 5 seconds of full attention when you store something.
- Avoid: Assume every lapse means a serious problem.
```

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-1)

---

<a id="book-1-recap"></a>
### 🟠 ⚡ 30-Second Recap

- Memory = encode, store, recall.
- Attention, sleep, exercise and calm help.
- Occasional forgetting is normal.

<a id="book-2-idea"></a>
### 🟢 💡 One-line Idea

> [!TIP]
> Memory is a trainable skill: turn information into vivid, strange images and place them along a familiar route.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-2)

---

<a id="book-2-lessons"></a>
### 🔵 🔑 Top 5 Key Lessons

> [!NOTE]
> 1. **Use a memory palace**
> 2. **Vivid, odd images stick**
> 3. **Memory skill is trained, not born**
> 4. **Practice with focus, not autopilot**
> 5. **Novelty makes life feel longer**

<details>
<summary>📖 Real-life examples (click to open)</summary>

1. *Put your grocery items in the rooms of your home.*
2. *Picture a giant egg smashing your front door for 'eggs'.*
3. *Champions started as average people.*
4. *You stop improving unless you push a little past comfort.*
5. *Try new routes and experiences so days don't blur together.*

</details>

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-2)

---

<a id="book-2-plan"></a>
### 🟡 ✅ 7-Day Action Plan

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Five small steps you can do this week.

| Day | Action | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Choose a familiar route with 10 stops. | ☐ |
| Day 2 | Memorize a 10-item list using that route. | ☐ |
| Day 3 | Use an image trick to remember 3 new names. | ☐ |
| Day 5 | Memorize the outline of a short talk or plan. | ☐ |
| Day 7 | Do one new activity and recall it in detail that night. | ☐ |

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-2)

---

<a id="book-2-habit"></a>
### 🟣 🔁 Daily Habit

> [!TIP]
> Each evening, store tomorrow's top 5 tasks along your memory route. Check them off the next day.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-2)

---

> [!WARNING]
> Using tricks for everything. They are best for lists and facts, not deep understanding.

```diff
+ Do: Make images vivid, funny and personal.
- Avoid: Use the same boring image again and again.
```

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-2)

---

<a id="book-2-recap"></a>
### 🟠 ⚡ 30-Second Recap

- Images plus a route make lists stick.

---

<a id="book-3-idea"></a>
### 🟢 💡 One-line Idea

> [!TIP]
> Memory fails in seven predictable ways (transience, absent-mindedness, blocking, misattribution, suggestibility, bias, persistence), so you can plan around them.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-3)

---

<a id="book-3-lessons"></a>
### 🔵 🔑 Top 5 Key Lessons

> [!NOTE]
> 1. **Transience: memories fade**
> 2. **Absent-mindedness: you never stored it**
> 3. **Blocking: tip-of-the-tongue**
> 4. **Misattribution: right fact, wrong source**
> 5. **Suggestibility and bias: memory is reshaped**

<details>
<summary>📖 Real-life examples (click to open)</summary>

1. *Review key things soon after learning them.*
2. *Use a cue, like a phone alarm or a note on the door.*
3. *Relax and move on; the word often pops up later.*
4. *Note where you heard important information.*
5. *Today's mood and leading questions change what you recall.*

</details>

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-3)

---

<a id="book-3-plan"></a>
### 🟡 ✅ 7-Day Action Plan

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Five small steps you can do this week.

| Day | Action | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Set one reminder for something you usually forget. | ☐ |
| Day 2 | Review today's notes tomorrow morning. | ☐ |
| Day 3 | When blocked on a word, stop forcing and come back in 10 minutes. | ☐ |
| Day 5 | Write the source next to one important fact. | ☐ |
| Day 7 | Write down an agreement instead of trusting memory. | ☐ |

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-3)

---

<a id="book-3-habit"></a>
### 🟣 🔁 Daily Habit

> [!TIP]
> Spend 2 minutes each evening writing the 3 most important things you learned or decided. Tick a calendar.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-3)

---

<a id="book-3-mistake"></a>
### 🔴 ⚠️ Common Mistake

> [!WARNING]
> Trusting memory blindly. Feeling certain does not mean you are accurate.

```diff
+ Do: Write down what matters.
- Avoid: Rely on 'I'll remember it'.
```

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-3)

---

<a id="book-3-recap"></a>
### 🟠 ⚡ 30-Second Recap

- Memory fails in 7 predictable ways.
- Cues, notes and review reduce failures.
- Feeling sure is not proof.

<a id="book-4-idea"></a>
### 🟢 💡 One-line Idea

> [!TIP]
> Memory is rebuilt every time you recall it, not played back like a video, so it can be distorted or even invented.

[⬆ Back to Section Contents](#book-4)

---

<a id="book-4-lessons"></a>
### 🔵 🔑 Top 5 Key Lessons

> [!NOTE]
> 1. **Recall is reconstruction**
> 2. **Confidence is not accuracy**
> 3. **Leading questions plant details**
> 4. **Imagining can create false memories**
> 5. **Retold stories drift**

<details>
<summary>📖 Real-life examples (click to open)</summary>

1. *Two siblings remember the same event differently.*
2. *You feel 100% sure and are still wrong.*
3. *'Was the car going fast?' changes the answer.*
4. *Repeatedly picturing an event can feel like it happened.*
5. *Each retelling changes small details.*

</details>
